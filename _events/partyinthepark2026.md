# Party in the Park - Community Resource Fair on September 12, 2026

For the past 3 years, over 1000 people met at Massachusetts Avenue Park. Join us this
year for Party in the Park at 11:00am on Saturday, September 12, 2026.
Community Canvases' [WNY Trash Mob](http://instagram.com/wnytrashmob/)
is organizing this Community Resource Fair in collaboration with
Assemblymember Jon Rivera, Councilmember David Rivera, and Buffalo Myanmar Festival. There will be
community, food, and lots of neighbors and local organizations.

Check out our **[Facebook event](https://www.facebook.com/events/2153003188593243)!**
